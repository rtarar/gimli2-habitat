# Decision: Bathroom Fitout

**ID:** DEC-007
**Date:** 2026-02-21
**Status:** FINAL

---

## Decisions

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Sink | **No bathroom sink** |
| Medicine cabinet | **Yes — included** |
| Towel warmer | **Yes — included** |

## No Sink

- Eliminates a dedicated plumbing branch (supply + drain) in the bathroom zone, reducing complexity, weight, and potential leak points.
- Handwashing done at the kitchen sink, which is already plumbed.
- Saves space in a compact wet-room.

**Implications:**
- No cold/hot supply lines to bathroom required.
- No bathroom drain or trap required.
- Wet-room wall finish does not need to accommodate a sink cutout.

## Medicine Cabinet

- Wall-mounted above the toilet or on the door-adjacent wall.
- Surface-mount preferred (avoids cutting into GRP sandwich panel).
- Must be secured per [DEC-005](DEC-005-grp-shell-attachment-method.md) attachment method (threaded inserts or backing plate).

## Towel Warmer

- Electric (not hydronic) — avoids adding a heating circuit branch to the bathroom plumbing.
- 12V DC or 230V AC depending on final electrical plan — confirm with [electrical layout](electrical/PLAN-electrical-layout.md).
- Wall-mounted; attachment per DEC-005.

## Scope

Applies to the bathroom/wet-room area only. Kitchen unaffected.
