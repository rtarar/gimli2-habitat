# Decision: Interior Wall Finish Strategy

**ID:** DEC-006
**Date:** 2026-02-21
**Status:** FINAL

---

## Context

The habitat uses GRP composite sandwich panels whose inner surface is GRP + gelcoat. The finish strategy must:

- Minimise condensation risk and cold corners
- Resist mildew/mold
- Have low water absorption
- Be wipe-clean
- Be repairable over long-term expedition vibration

## Decision

1. **Primary wall/ceiling finish:** Paint directly on the interior GRP/gelcoat. No additional wall liner panels or full-wall wallpaper.
2. **Kitchen backsplash zone only:** Use fiberglass wallpaper (glass-fiber wall covering) in the backsplash area, then paint over it (or use as finished surface per product spec).

## Rationale

- Painted GRP is non-organic, low absorption, and easy to clean. Avoids creating hidden cavities where moisture can be trapped.
- Keeping the finish simple reduces the chance of mildew/mold growth behind coverings.
- Fiberglass wallpaper in the backsplash provides extra impact/scratch resistance and a replaceable reinforcement layer in the highest splash/abuse zone, while keeping the rest of the cabin moisture-safe.

## Build Notes

- Painting GRP requires proper prep: **clean/de-wax → sand for tooth → epoxy primer (if needed) → durable topcoat**
- Use **satin/eggshell** sheen where possible — better defect hiding while remaining wipeable
- Backsplash wallpaper adhesive/primer must be compatible with GRP and the chosen paint system

## Scope

All interior wall/ceiling surfaces in the habitat, **except** the kitchen backsplash area.
